Questions

Things clients usually ask.

Something else on your mind? Message us on WhatsApp and we'll answer directly.

How much does a website or system cost?

It depends on what you need. After a short conversation we send a clear, itemised quote, so you know the full cost before any work starts.

How long does a project take?

A business website usually takes a few weeks; a custom system takes longer depending on its size. Your quote includes a timeline we commit to.

Can you work with the system we already have?

Often, yes. We can connect to existing tools, move your current records into a new system, or improve what you already use.

Do your systems support M-Pesa?

Yes. Our POS takes M-Pesa payments, and we can add M-Pesa to custom systems and websites.

Do you work with clients outside Kenya?

Yes. We work remotely with clients anywhere, using calls, WhatsApp and shared previews throughout the project.

What happens after the project is finished?

You can choose ongoing support: hosting, fixes, updates, training and improvements whenever you need them.
