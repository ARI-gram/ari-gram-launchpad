About ARI-GRAM

We build the software Kenyan businesses run on.

A technology company focused on one thing: helping businesses operate better, manage their work and grow, with software that fits how they actually work.

Our story

From websites to the systems behind the business.

ARI-GRAM started by building websites for local businesses. Working closely with those owners, we kept seeing the same thing: the real problems weren't on the website. They were in the stock book, the payroll spreadsheet, the purchase orders waiting for a signature.

So we started building the systems for that too. Today ARI-GRAM designs and supports business software (point of sale, payroll and HR, construction materials management) alongside the websites and brand work our clients still rely on us for.
