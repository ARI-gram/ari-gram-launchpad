01 · Who we are

A software company that learns how your business works first.

ARI-GRAM Technologies builds business software and digital platforms for companies in Kenya and beyond. We start with the way your team already works (the stock sheets, the payroll spreadsheet, the order book) and turn it into a system that is faster and easier to trust.

Websites, branding and support are part of what we do, but our focus is the software that runs the business behind them.

1

Built around real operations

We study the workflow before writing code, so the system fits the business, not the other way round.

2

Made for the Kenyan market

M-Pesa payments, KRA requirements, PAYE, NSSF and SHIF built in where they apply.

3

We stay after launch

Training, maintenance and improvements once your team is using the system day to day.
